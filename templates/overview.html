How the agent works

  1. Data

    SAP-like purchase orders, suppliers, deliveries and stock are generated, validated and cleaned.

  2. Risk engine

    Business rules and the supplier's historical late rate give a composite risk; a priority score ranks the orders.

  3. Agent

    Investigates the top orders with mock SAP tools and recommends actions. Sensitive actions wait for your approval.

  4. Audit

    Every tool call, decision and approval is written to an append-only log.
